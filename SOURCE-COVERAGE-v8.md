## Material corrections and safeguards

- **PDPA:** the rebuild uses the current PDPC obligation framework. It does not claim that a generic consent checkbox makes all health-data use lawful or fair.
- **Employment Act:** statutory sick-leave administration is distinguished from a blanket right to diagnoses or unrelated health data.
- **Workplace fairness:** future Singapore requirements are labelled as future implementation, not current in-force duties.
- **Mental health:** the named conditions retained from the legacy deck are used to teach “respond without diagnosing”; managers observe work-related change, ask privately, support and escalate safely.
- **Resilience:** individual capability is paired with job design, manager behaviour, social resources and professional support. The course rejects a “toughen up” interpretation.
- **Measurement:** participation, absence and productivity are treated as signals with selection and confounding limits, not automatic proof of programme causation or ROI.

## Added evidence base

The course integrates the nine sources requested by the course owner plus current Singapore sources from PDPC, TAFEP, HPB, MOM and Healthier SG. Source URLs appear in the Learner Guide, evidence-source slides and per-slide speaker-note `[Sources]` blocks.
